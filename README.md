# Prime-shift extensions of Kaplansky–Riordan's ménage identities: Mathar's recurrence and van Hoeij's congruences

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending Kaplansky and Riordan's ménage identities (1946, pp. 121–122, equations (25) and (33)), we use Jovović's factorial-series generating function to prove the conjectures of Mathar (recurrence) and van Hoeij (prime-shift congruence) in OEIS A000159, revision 54, first and third FORMULA lines, dated 2 November 2015 and 10 June 2019. For the hit numbers u_h(n)=[t^h]U_n(t), the stronger uniform result is u_h(n)+2u_h(n+p)+u_h(n+2p) ≡ 0 (mod p) for every prime p, 1 <= h < p and n >= 0; the zero-hit case holds for n >= 1. It settles the neighboring two-, four- and five-hit congruences. We also prove prime-square antiperiodicity for three hits, give exact recurrence certificates for the neighboring diagonals, and correct the one-hit indexing formula by identifying it with a classical identity.
